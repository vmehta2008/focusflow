      ⚠️ Read this before you paste a key

      • The key is saved on this device only — never uploaded, never in the file you share.
      • Do not put a key in the file you upload to GitHub. The page is public; anyone could read it and use your quota.

      • Your question and a summary of your study data go to the AI provider. Nothing else is sent.
      Why two. They are good at different things, and the app sends each job to whichever suits it. 🔍 Gemini is the only one that can search the live web while it answers — so syllabus checks and exam-board news always go to it. 🧠 Claude reasons through a concept better — so "explain why depreciation is charged" goes to it, and the answer is a teacher's, not a summary's. 🗺 ChatGPT lays out a mindmap more cleanly. Given two, hard questions get answered by both and reconciled — and where they disagree on a figure or a section number, you're shown both rather than one being quietly picked. Cost, honestly: Gemini has a free tier. Claude and ChatGPT do not — they bill per message, usually a fraction of a rupee each, but it is a card on file.
