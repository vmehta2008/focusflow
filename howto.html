The idea

Two halves of one thing

The app holds everything. The bot carries it to where you already are at eleven at night.

📱 The app
Everything lives here
Your chapters, hours, notes, marks. Nothing leaves your device unless you say so.
✈️ The bot
Keeps up with you
Asks each morning. Answers doubts at midnight. Messages you if you go quiet.
The one rule worth knowing.
The bot only knows what the app last told it — and the app tells it when you open it. Open the app once a day and the bot stays sharp. It will say so out loud if its information is stale, rather than bluffing.

Do these five things

The whole thing, honestly

If you only take five instructions from this, take these.

1
Open the app once a day. That's what keeps the bot current. Everything vague it ever says traces back to this.
2
Put your chapters in. All of them. Paste the syllabus. The app is only as clever as its list.
3
Record every test score, including the bad ones. Especially the bad ones — that's the only way it can find your weak chapters.
4
Answer the morning check-in. Four taps. It's the difference between a diary and a coach.
5
Press 💾 in the top bar every couple of weeks. Your data lives in this browser only. A browser clean-up would take all of it.
Nothing here is uploaded anywhere. No account, no password, no server holding your marks. That's deliberate — and it's also why the backup is on you.

Getting the Telegram bot

Ask whoever sent you this. The bot needs a one-off setup that only has to be done once for everybody — they'll send you a link. Tap it, answer three questions, and you're in.
Then check it. Message the bot /help. Six buttons should appear at the bottom of the chat. Tap 📊 My week — that one works even if everything else is misconfigured, so it's the honest first test.
You don't have to. Every part of the app works without it. The bot is the difference between an app you remember to open and one that keeps up with you — but it's an addition, not a requirement.

If something looks wrong

The bot sounds generic. It hasn't had fresh data. Open the app once — that's the whole fix. Ask it and it'll tell you how old its information is.
The bot mentions an AI key. That's the server, not you. Tell whoever set it up. 📊 My week still works without it.
An answer looks wrong. Every answer is checked by a second model. Look for the ⚠ line — that's where the two disagreed, and it's the line to trust least.
Everything vanished. Restore from your last 💾 backup. If you never made one, it's gone — nothing is stored on a server. So make one now, honestly.

Three honest limits

It doesn't study for you. It removes the deciding, the forgetting and the guessing. The sitting down is still yours.
The AI can be wrong. That's why two models check each other and why disagreements are shown rather than hidden. Treat a section number or a date as a lead, not a fact.
Your data is only on your device. No account, no password, nobody holding your marks. That's the point — and it's why the 💾 button matters.
